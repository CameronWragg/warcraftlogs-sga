THE SMALL PRINT

How this was counted

• Every boss pull in the raid at any difficulty; trash counts only for The Janitor; dungeons and Mythic+ left out.

• Pulls logged by several people are merged: the highest value per player per metric, never added together.

• Wipe call: Warcraft Logs' own call time, else 10 seconds before the wipe ended. Deaths after it don't count for first-to-die.

• Awards go to regulars: anyone at 10%+ of pulls.

Source data: the season's gold ledgers (pulls.csv, player_pulls.csv) and stats.json.
